# Two-factor and passkeys

Two-factor authentication is **required for everyone** who signs in with a password or LDAP. It's in **Settings → Sign-in → Two-factor authentication**. (People who use single sign-on get two-factor from their identity provider instead.)

## Authenticator app

You set one up the first time you sign in, by scanning a QR code. Any standard app works.

**Set up a new authenticator** (for a new phone): enter your password, scan the new code, and enter a code from the new app to confirm. Your old app keeps working until you confirm. You get new recovery codes too.

## Recovery codes

Ten codes, each usable **once** in place of a code. You get them at your first sign-in. **Make new recovery codes** (after entering your password) replaces them; the old ones stop working at once. The console warns you when few are left, and you're emailed when one is used.

## Passkeys and security keys

A passkey (on your laptop or phone) or a security key (such as a YubiKey) can be used **instead of the 6-digit code**. They can't be phished: your device signs only for this site.

<Steps>

### Add one

Select **Add a passkey or security key**. Give it a name you'll recognise, such as "Work laptop", and enter your password.

<Shot src="/screenshots/passkey-add.png" alt="The Add a passkey or security key dialog, with a name and password" width="760px" />

### Confirm on your device

Your browser asks you to create a passkey, with your fingerprint, face, PIN or by touching your key.

### Use it

Next time you sign in, at the code step, select **Use a passkey**.

</Steps>

Your passkeys are listed with when they were added and last used. **Remove** one at any time. You can have up to 10. Adding one emails you a notice.

<Callout type="info">
Passkeys stand in for the code, never the password: you still enter your password (or LDAP password) first.
</Callout>

## For admins

- **Reset two-factor** on a person's page (under People) clears their authenticator, recovery codes and passkeys. They set up again at their next sign-in. It needs another admin; nobody can reset themselves.
