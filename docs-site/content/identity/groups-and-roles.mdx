# Groups and roles

Map a group at your identity provider to roles in Onshore. Once any group is mapped, **people's roles follow their groups** each time they sign in or are synced. This works the same for [single sign-on](/identity/single-sign-on/), [SCIM](/identity/scim/) and [LDAP](/identity/ldap/), and in every edition.

<Steps>

### Add a group

In **Settings → Sign-in → Groups and roles**, select **Add a group**.

### Name it

Type the group's name, such as `Onshore-Admins`, or for Entra ID, the group's ID. Names match without regard to case.

### Choose roles

Tick the roles members get. A person in several mapped groups gets every role from all of them.

### Save

Select **Save mappings**.

</Steps>

## What changes

| | Without mappings | With mappings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roles | Set by hand on the People page | Follow groups at every sign-in or sync |
| Someone with no mapped group | Signs in with whatever roles they have | **Can't get in**, and loses their roles |
| New people (with account creation on) | Not created | Created with their groups' roles |

<Callout type="warning">
**The last admin is never removed this way.** If a group change would leave your organisation with no active admin, that person keeps the admin role.
</Callout>

## Tips

- Create dedicated groups such as `Onshore-Admins`, `Onshore-DPO`, `Onshore-Support`, rather than reusing broad ones.
- Remove someone from Onshore by removing them from its groups at your provider: their roles go at their next sign-in or sync. With SCIM, deactivating them ends their sessions immediately.
- Roles are listed in [People and roles](/guides/people-and-roles/#roles).
