# Your first connection

The quickest way to see Onshore working is to connect your logs. The **setup guide** (Home → setup guide) walks you through it with a test vault, so nothing touches live data.

<Shot src="/screenshots/onboarding.png" alt="The setup guide with steps to create a test vault and connect a log collector" />

<Steps>

### Create a test vault

Select **Create test vault**. Test vaults hold made-up data only; you can try values in them freely.

### Say where your logs go today

Enter your log vendor's address under **Destination URL** (for example your Datadog, Grafana Cloud or Elastic intake), then select **Connect, watching only**.

### Add Onshore to your collector

The guide shows **Collector settings** for OpenTelemetry Collector, Fluent Bit or Vector: a new endpoint and a key. Add them to your collector, then select **I have added it**. Details: [Log pipelines](/developers/log-pipelines/).

### Watch

The connection shows **Watching. Nothing has changed yet**. Your logs reach your vendor exactly as before, and Onshore counts what it would have replaced. After a day or two, look at the counts on the [Connections](/guides/connections/) page.

### Turn protection on

When the counts look right, open the connection and select **Turn protection on**. From then on, identifiers in your logs reach your vendor as tokens.

</Steps>
