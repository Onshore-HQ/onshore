# Any other country

Every country can be chosen at setup and for a vault: **243 in all**. In setup, countries without a built-in pack are listed under **Other countries**, labelled **No built-in pack: make your own**.

A vault in such a country:

- finds everything in the **Everywhere** pack: names, emails, cards, amounts, international phone numbers;
- has its date and number formats filled in from the browser's own data for that country;
- has no suggested laws.

To add the rest:

1. Make [your own country pack](/guides/country-packs/) with the country's IDs, account numbers and laws, or import a pack file a partner or the community shares.
2. Test it, apply it to the vault, and have a second person approve each data type.

Good community packs can be reviewed and added to the built-in list in a future release.
