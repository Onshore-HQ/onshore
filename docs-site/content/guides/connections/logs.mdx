# Logs

Application logs are full of account numbers, phone numbers and ID numbers, and they ship abroad all day. The **log processor** sits between your log collector and your log vendor, and replaces identifiers with tokens on the way through. Your applications don't change.

<Shot src="/screenshots/connections-logs.png" alt="The Logs tab of Connections, with a log connection, its actions per type of data, and its settings" />

## Connect your logs

<Steps>

### Start a log connection

In **Connections**, select **Add a connection**, then **Logs**. Or use the **Logs** tab.

### Fill in the form

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Vault** | Where tokens are stored. Usually the vault for the country your logs come from |
| **Name** | For you, such as "Payments API logs" |
| **Source** | OpenTelemetry Collector, Fluent Bit or Vector |
| **Destination name** | Your log vendor, such as Datadog |
| **Destination URL** | Your vendor's intake address, starting `https://` |

Select **Create**.

### Add the settings to your collector

You get **Collector settings**: a new endpoint and a **pipeline key**, shown once. Add them to your collector's configuration. Examples for each collector are in [Log pipelines](/developers/log-pipelines/).

### Watch, then protect

Logs pass through **unchanged** while the connection is watch only, and Onshore counts what it would replace. When ready, select **Turn protection on**.

</Steps>

## What happens to each type of data

For each **type of data**, choose **what to do with it**:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Tokenize** | The value becomes a token. With "same value, same token" fields, you can still follow one customer through your logs |
| **Mask** | Most of the value is hidden, such as `*******6789` |
| **Drop the line** | The whole line is dropped |
| **Pass and count** | Sent unchanged, and counted |

Card numbers are always masked, never stored in the vault.

## If the vault is unreachable

- **Buffer in your collector and retry** (recommended): the processor tells the collector to try again later, so nothing is lost or leaked.
- **Drop the logs**: lines are discarded.

<Callout type="warning">
When protecting, **unredacted logs are never forwarded**, whichever you choose.
</Callout>

## Fields that are always sensitive

Under **Always-sensitive JSON paths**, list fields that must be protected whatever they contain, one per line, as `path:type`, for example `$.customer.bvn:bvn`.

## Try it

Paste a sample line (made-up data only) and select **Show what would be forwarded**. You see the line as your vendor would receive it once protection is on. Tokens shown are examples; nothing is stored.

## Keys

**Rotate pipeline key** issues a new key, shown once. Update your collector; the old key stops working. A wrong key is refused.

## Payment references

Payment references may count as payment data under some rules, but they are poor clues for anything else, so the `payment_ref` type is **off** by default. Turn it on per pipeline if your counsel says so.
