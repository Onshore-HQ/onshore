# Settings

**Settings** has six tabs. Most need the admin role.

## Organisation

<Shot src="/screenshots/settings-org.png" alt="Settings, Organisation: name, hosting facility, country and licence" />

- **Organisation** name, and **hosting facility** (the facility name and city, as it should appear in the residency report; record the real one).
- **Country**: set when the install was installed, and fixed.
- **Licence**: customer, expiry and validity. An expired licence keeps the product running; upgrades stop.

## Locations and laws

<Shot src="/screenshots/settings-locations-full.png" alt="Settings, Locations and laws: where your data lives, where your customers live, laws that apply, and organisation and display settings" />

### Where your data lives

Each vault's country (fixed), its country packs and its **time zone** (inherits your organisation's unless set). Add or remove country packs for a vault. With Enterprise or Cloud, this tab also shows [linked installs and Cloud regions](/guides/linked-installs/).

### Where your customers live

The places your customers live, which suggest extra laws and packs.

### Laws that apply

Each law with why it's suggested, a summary, its source and when it was last checked. Remove one, or **Add another law**. Laws from your own [country packs](/guides/country-packs/) are listed under **From your country packs**.

### Organisation and display

Your country, sector, **console language** (English or French), how **dates and numbers** look, and your organisation's **time zone**. The time zone decides where a day ends for usage, reports and the map, and office-hours access rules.

Select **Save** to apply, or **Discard**.

## Sign-in

<Shot src="/screenshots/settings-signin.png" alt="Settings, Sign-in: change password, two-factor authentication and passkeys, and single sign-on" />

- **Change password** (signs you out everywhere).
- **Two-factor authentication**: make new recovery codes, set up a new authenticator, and add or remove **passkeys and security keys**. See [Two-factor and passkeys](/identity/two-factor-and-passkeys/).
- For admins: **single sign-on**, **groups and roles**, **directory sync (SCIM)** and **LDAP directory**. See [Sign-in and identity](/identity/).

## Email

<Shot src="/screenshots/settings-email.png" alt="Settings, Email: your mail server settings" />

Onshore sends email through **your own mail server**; there is no Onshore mail service. It sends invitations, password resets, notices about account changes and alert digests. They hold staff names and links, **never customer data**.

Enter the **mail server** and **port**, the **security** (STARTTLS, usual; TLS from the start; or none, for an internal relay only), an optional username and password, the **send from** address and sender name, and **this console's address** (used in email links). Optionally tick **Email new alerts to admins and DPOs**. Select **Save**, then **Send a test**. A password is refused without a secure connection. The mail server may be private, but can't be on Onshore's own networks.

## Copy setup

<Shot src="/screenshots/settings-copy.png" alt="Settings, Copy setup: export and import setup between installs" />

Move your setup from a test install to a live one, or between linked installs: fields, your own data types, access rules, approved networks and connections. It never holds keys, secrets, people or customer data.

<Steps>

### Export

Under **Export**, choose the **vault to export** and select **Download setup file**.

### Import, as a dry run

On the other install, under **Import**, choose the **vault to import into** and the **setup file**, and select **See what would change**. Nothing is applied yet; each item shows its result, and anything it won't touch is marked **Won't change**.

### Apply

Importing is deliberately cautious:

- Access rules and data types arrive as **drafts**; a second person publishes them.
- New connections start as **watch only**, with new keys.
- A live connection is never redirected, switched to another mode or weakened.
- A field's token type never changes.
- **Nothing is ever deleted.**

</Steps>

**Keys for pipelines** lets a CI pipeline do this: select **New key**, choose **what it can do** and when it expires (30 days, 90 days or a year). The key is shown once, acts as the admin who made it, and stops working if that person loses the role. See [Control API](/developers/control-api/#copy-setup).

## Audit records

<Shot src="/screenshots/settings-audit.png" alt="Settings, Audit records: retention and SIEM endpoint" />

- **Keep audit events for**, in days (at least 365; seven years by default).
- **SIEM endpoint**: an `https` address to send a copy of every audit event to. A signing secret is shown once; each batch carries `X-RV-Signature: sha256=…` over its body.

<Callout type="warning">
A SIEM copy is itself data leaving Onshore. Point it at a destination in the same country as your vaults.
</Callout>
