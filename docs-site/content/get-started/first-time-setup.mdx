# First-time setup

The first time an admin signs in, the console opens **first-time setup**. It takes five steps and creates your vaults at the end. Nothing is created until the last step, and you can go back at any time.

Setup is **pre-filled from where you are**: it guesses your country from your browser's time zone and language, then fills in each step to suit it. You confirm or change everything.

<Steps>

### Your organisation

<Shot src="/screenshots/setup-1.png" alt="Setup step 1: Your organisation, with the detected country, organisation name, sector and display settings" />

- **Your country**: where your company is. Setup explains how it guessed. Change it if it's wrong.
- **Organisation name**: shown in the console and on reports.
- **Sector**: decides which industry rules are suggested later, such as payment data rules for banking.
- **How the console looks to you**: language (English or French) and how dates and numbers are written. Display only: it doesn't decide where data is kept.

### Where data stays

<Shot src="/screenshots/setup-2.png" alt="Setup step 2: a grid of countries with a Full data pack label, and the install's country selected" />

Each country you pick becomes a **vault**. Pick where your customers' data must live, which isn't always where your company is based.

- Countries with a **Full data pack** have built-in detection for their IDs and accounts.
- **Other countries** are all listed too. A country with no built-in pack still gets a vault that finds names, emails, cards, amounts and phone numbers; after setup you can add its IDs as your own [country pack](/guides/country-packs/).
- On a self-hosted install, the install's country is always included and can't be removed.
- **Free** keeps one country per install. **Enterprise** plans an install in each other country; **Onshore Cloud** sets up a region where one is open, or puts the country on a waitlist.

At the bottom, confirm that you understand a vault's country is final:

<Shot src="/screenshots/setup-2-confirm.png" alt="The confirmation that a vault's country cannot change" />

### Laws

<Shot src="/screenshots/setup-3.png" alt="Setup step 3: suggested laws, each with the reasons it was suggested and a source link" />

Setup suggests the laws to track, from your countries, sector and the places your customers live. Each shows **why** it was suggested, a short summary, its **source**, and when we last checked it. Untick any that don't apply, or add others.

<Callout type="info">
Law summaries are a starting point, not legal advice. Your counsel decides what applies.
</Callout>

### Personal data

<Shot src="/screenshots/setup-4.png" alt="Setup step 4: the Everywhere pack and the country pack, each listing the data types it finds with examples" />

Each **country pack** teaches Onshore that country's ID numbers, phone numbers and bank account formats. The packs for your vaults and customers are switched on. The **Everywhere** pack is always on. Add any other country your systems see under **Add another country pack**.

Everything starts in **watch only**: Onshore counts what it finds and changes nothing until you turn protection on for each connection.

### Check and create

<Shot src="/screenshots/setup-5.png" alt="Setup step 5: a summary of the vaults, laws and packs that will be created" />

Review what will be created, then select **Create 1 vault** (the button counts your vaults). Nothing is created until you press it. Afterwards you see:

- the vaults created now;
- for Enterprise, a **join command** for each other country, to run on a server there (valid for 24 hours, single use);
- for Cloud, the regions being set up and any countries on the waitlist.

</Steps>

## After setup

- Change anything except a vault's country in **Settings → Locations and laws**. See [Settings](/guides/settings/).
- Follow the **setup guide** on the home page for a first connection: [Your first connection](/get-started/first-connection/).
- Invite your team: [People and roles](/guides/people-and-roles/).
