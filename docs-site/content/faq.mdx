# FAQ

## About the product

**Does Onshore make us compliant?**
No product can do that alone. Onshore gives you controls and evidence; your counsel decides what your obligations are.

**Do we still need a database in the country?**
Usually, where a local rule requires it. Onshore handles identifiers and the flows around your database; the rest of your data still needs a home in the country.

**What happens to our tools abroad?**
They keep working, on tokens. Log searches still work with "same value, same token" fields; AI models reason about placeholders; helpdesks show tokens or masked values with a reveal for permitted agents.

**Which countries are supported?**
18 have built-in packs; every other country can have a vault and your own country pack. See [Countries](/countries/).

**Is it open source?**
Yes. Free is fully self-hosted. Enterprise adds linked installs, single sign-on, SCIM, LDAP and support. Onshore Cloud is run by us.

**Does it phone home?**
No. Self-hosted installs use an offline licence, and services reach only the destinations you configure.

## Admins

**Can admins see customer values?**
Not by default. Only roles an access rule allows can reveal values. The starter rules let the DPO reveal, with multi-factor sign-in and a purpose.

**What if we lose the key shares?**
If three or more of the five are lost, stored values can never be decrypted again. Keep them safe and apart.

**How do we remove someone's access quickly?**
Suspend them on the People page, or with SCIM, deactivate them at your identity provider: their sessions end at once.

**Can we try it safely?**
Yes: use a test vault with made-up data, and keep every connection in watch only until you trust the counts.

## Developers

**Which languages have SDKs?**
Node.js, Python, Go, Java and PHP on the server; web, iOS, Android and React Native for secure components.

**Do we need to change our app to protect logs?**
No. Change one output in your log collector.

**What if Onshore is down?**
Detokenize fails closed. Logs buffer in your collector and retry (if you choose that). Nothing unprotected is forwarded while protecting.

**Can tokens look like real values?**
Yes, with format-preserving tokens, for old systems that check length. Prefer opaque tokens otherwise.
