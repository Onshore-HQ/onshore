# Home

**Home** is the first page after sign-in. It answers two questions: *what needs my attention?* and *what should I do next?*

<Shot src="/screenshots/overview.png" alt="Home, with Needs your attention, four summary cards, and the chart of values caught before leaving the country" />

## Needs your attention

Problems that need a person, newest first. Select one to go straight to it. Typical items:

- **Unusual access alerts**: someone viewed more real values than is normal for them. See [People and roles](/guides/people-and-roles/#unusual-access).
- **Map alerts**: a flow changed status, or something on the residency map needs a decision.
- **Leaking flows**: sensitive values are leaving the country unprotected on these paths.

## Get set up

Until you've finished, Home shows a checklist with your progress:

1. **Create a vault**: where real values live, in your vault's country.
2. **Connect your first source**: logs, an AI tool, a vendor API or a support tool. Or **Open the setup guide** for a guided first connection.
3. **Check your connections and turn protection on**: once you trust what you see.
4. **Load your existing customers**: [import data](/guides/import-data/).
5. **Complete your residency map**: add systems Onshore can't see.
6. **Decide who can see real values**: write an [access rule](/guides/access-rules/). Publishing needs a second person.
7. **Download your first report**: signed evidence for your compliance officer.

Select **Hide this** to put the checklist away.

## The summary cards

| Card | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Connections** | How many connections send data through Onshore, how many are protecting, and how many flows on your map are checked by their own jobs or not connected yet |
| **Values protected** | Values held in the vault, and for how many people |
| **Real values viewed, last 7 days** | Full reveals, masked views, refusals and open alerts |
| **Response time** | How fast 95% of requests were answered in the last hour, and whether the key service is healthy |

## Caught before leaving the country

Sensitive values found each day over the last 14 days, by type. It includes values from watch-only connections, which were counted but not changed. Choose a destination to narrow it, and switch between **Chart** and **Table**.
