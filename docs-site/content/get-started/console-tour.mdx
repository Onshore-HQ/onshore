# Console tour

<Shot src="/screenshots/overview.png" alt="The console with the menu on the left grouped into Start, See, Protect, Control, Prove and Admin" />

The menu on the left is grouped by the job you came to do:

| Group | Pages | What you do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start** | [Home](/guides/home/) | See what needs attention, and what to do next |
| **See** | [Residency map](/guides/residency-map/) | See where your data goes, and what still leaks |
| **Protect** | [Connections](/guides/connections/), [Data types](/guides/data-types/), [Vaults](/guides/vaults/), [Redact a file](/guides/redact-a-file/) | Put Onshore in the path of your data, and decide what counts as sensitive |
| **Control** | [Customer records](/guides/customer-records/), [People](/guides/people-and-roles/), [Access rules](/guides/access-rules/) | Find a person's data, manage your team, decide who sees real values |
| **Prove** | [Reports](/guides/reports/), [Audit log](/guides/audit-log/) | Produce signed evidence and answer "who saw what" |
| **Admin** | [Encryption keys](/guides/encryption-keys/), [Usage](/guides/usage-and-service-logs/), [Service logs](/guides/usage-and-service-logs/), [Health & alerts](/guides/health-and-alerts/), [Settings](/guides/settings/) | Keys, measurements, service health, organisation settings |

You see only what your role allows. A support agent, for example, sees fewer pages than an admin.

## Finding a page

Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows, Linux), or select **Go to a page…** at the top, and type what you want: "keys", "invite", "import".

<Shot src="/screenshots/search.png" alt="The Go to a page search, filtering pages as you type" />

## Help on every page

Every page has **What is this?** at the top right. It opens a short explanation of the page and links to these docs.

## The top bar

- **Demo organisation** appears when the install runs the demo data. Real installs don't show it.
- The moon icon switches between light and dark.
- Your name at the bottom left opens your account: your details, sign-out and language.

## Language

The console speaks **English and French**. An admin sets the organisation's language in **Settings → Locations and laws**.

<Shot src="/screenshots/french-overview.png" alt="The console home page in French" />

The French translation hasn't yet been reviewed by a native speaker. Text sent by the server, such as law descriptions and error messages, emails and the PDF report, stays in English for now.
