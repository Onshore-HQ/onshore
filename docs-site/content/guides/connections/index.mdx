# Connections

A **connection** puts Onshore in the path of data that would otherwise leave the country: your logs on their way to a log vendor, prompts on their way to an AI model, requests to a vendor API, tickets in a helpdesk. **Connections** lists them all, with what each has caught.

<Shot src="/screenshots/connections.png" alt="Connections: tabs for All, Logs, AI, Web APIs, Support tools and Page protection, a summary line, and a card per connection with its status and a 30-day chart" />

The summary line shows how many connections there are, how many are protecting, how many are watch only, and how many values were caught in 30 days. **flows on the map** opens the [residency map](/guides/residency-map/).

Each card shows the connection's kind and destination, its status, a 30-day chart of values caught, and when it last saw traffic.

## Watch only, then protecting

<Callout type="info">
Every connection **starts as watch only**: it counts what would have left the country, and changes nothing. When the counts look right, open it and select **Turn protection on**. **Back to watch only** reverses it, and the residency report records both.
</Callout>

## Add a connection

Select **Add a connection** to choose what to protect.

Each choice says **what it stops**, **what you need**, and **what happens**:

| Choice | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Logs, error tools (OpenTelemetry, Fluent Bit, Vector) | [Logs](/guides/connections/logs/) |
| AI tools and chatbots | [AI gateway](/guides/connections/ai-gateway/) |
| Vendor and partner APIs | [Web APIs](/guides/connections/http-proxy/) |
| Freshdesk, Zoho Desk, Salesforce, Intercom | [Support tools](/guides/connections/support-tools/) |
| Checkout and sign-up pages | [Page protection](/guides/connections/page-protection/) |
| A job that copies data abroad | [Flow checks](/developers/data-api/#flow-checks) |

## Legal ground

Every log pipeline, AI route, proxy route and support tool has a **legal ground for sending this data abroad**, which appears in the residency report:

- Not assessed yet (the default)
- Tokens only: counsel advises no personal data leaves
- Adequacy decision
- Instrument approved by your data protection authority
- Another lawful ground for transfer

Onshore doesn't choose a ground for you: record what your counsel advised.
