# Reports

**Reports** produces signed evidence of where your data lives, what's protected, what still flows abroad and who saw what. Reports are **computed from the audit log**, never from separate counters, so every number traces back to recorded events.

<Shot src="/screenshots/reports.png" alt="Reports: a date range, the report preview with its sections, and Download signed PDF and Download CSV" />

## Make a report

<Steps>

### Choose the period

Pick the **From** and **To** dates. Days end in your organisation's time zone.

### Preview it

The preview shows every section, below.

### Download

**Download signed PDF** for people; **Download CSV** for spreadsheets and governance tools.

</Steps>

## What's in it

| Section | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Cover** | Your organisation, the period, who generated it, and the **signature fingerprint** |
| **2. Data location** | Each vault's country and hosting facility, where keys are held and who administers them |
| **3. What is protected** | Values held per data type, and for how many people |
| **4. Outbound flows** | Each connection's destination, what was protected, what **passed while watch only**, and its legal ground |
| **5. Access** | Reveals by role and result, **denied attempts**, **break-glass events**, and reveals not on an approved network |
| **6. Changes** | Access rule and data type changes, with who approved each; key rotations |
| **7. Integrity** | Whether the audit chain verified, how many events, and how many **signed checkpoints** cover the period |
| **8. Data subject requests** | Exports and erasures |

## Your scope definition

Rules such as "payment data must stay in the country" often don't define exactly what counts. Record **what your company counts** as in-scope data, as your counsel advised, under the report: type it and select **Adopt as a new version**. Each version is dated and kept. The residency map and reports use the current one.

<Callout type="info">
Reports give you evidence to work with. Whether that evidence satisfies a regulator is for your compliance team and counsel.
</Callout>

## Checking a report

The PDF is signed with your organisation's audit key. The fingerprint on the cover lets anyone check it wasn't altered. See [Audit log](/guides/audit-log/#checking-the-chain).
