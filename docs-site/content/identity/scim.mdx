# SCIM provisioning <Ent />

With **SCIM 2.0**, your identity provider adds people to Onshore, keeps their names and emails up to date, pushes groups, and **removes people when they leave**: their access ends immediately. It works with Entra ID, Okta, OneLogin, JumpCloud, authentik and any SCIM 2.0 client.

SCIM usually goes with [single sign-on](/identity/single-sign-on/): SCIM manages accounts, single sign-on lets people in.

## Set it up

<Steps>

### Make a token

In **Settings → Sign-in → Directory sync (SCIM)**, select **Make a SCIM token** and name it after the provider it's for. Copy the **SCIM URL** and **token**: the token is shown once. You can have five active tokens.

The SCIM URL looks like `https://vault.yourbank.example/control/scim/v2`.

### Give them to your provider

Paste the URL and token into your provider's provisioning settings, as a bearer token. See [Provider guides](/identity/providers/).

### Map groups

Map the groups your provider pushes to roles in [Groups and roles](/identity/groups-and-roles/).

### Start provisioning

Turn provisioning on at your provider, ideally for a test group first.

</Steps>

The card shows how many people are synced, and each token's last use.

## What your provider can do

| Action | Effect in Onshore |
| --- | --- |
| Create a user | An account is created, marked "synced by SCIM". It has no password: the person signs in with single sign-on |
| Update a user | Name, email and external ID are updated |
| Deactivate a user (`active: false`) | The account is suspended and **every session ends at once** |
| Reactivate | The account is active again |
| Delete a user | The account is **removed**: kept for the audit trail, with no roles, and can never sign in. Pushing the same person again restores the same account |
| Create, update or delete a group | Members' roles are recalculated from the mapped groups |

Lookups by `userName`, `externalId` or email, and by group `displayName`, are supported, as are the PATCH styles Entra ID and Okta send (including Entra's `"False"` as a string). Every change is in the audit log.

<Callout type="warning">
SCIM **can't deactivate or delete your last admin**: the request is refused with a message your provider shows.
</Callout>

## Revoking a token

Select **Revoke** next to a token. Your provider stops syncing until you give it a new one. People already synced keep their access.
