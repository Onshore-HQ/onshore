# Laws

Setup suggests laws from your vaults' countries, your sector and where your customers live. Each law shows **why** it was suggested, a summary, its source, and the date we last checked it (**21 November 2026**). You can remove any, or add others, in **Settings → Locations and laws**.

<Callout type="warning">
These summaries are a starting point, **not legal advice**. Read each source, and let your counsel decide what applies to you.
</Callout>

## How suggestions are made

| Suggested when | Laws |
| --- | --- |
| A vault is in the country | That country's law(s) |
| Sector is banking and a vault is in Nigeria | CBN payments rule |
| Sector is banking and you serve people in the EU | DORA |
| Sector is banking and a vault is in the US | GLBA |
| Sector is health and a vault is in the US | HIPAA |
| You serve people in a place (Settings → Locations and laws) | That place's law |

## Your own laws

Laws for other countries, or ones the catalogue doesn't have, can be added in [your own country pack](/guides/country-packs/), with their source and the date you checked them.

## What Onshore does about each law

Onshore doesn't decide whether a transfer is lawful. It gives you:

- a **legal ground** field on every connection, which appears in the residency report;
- **evidence**: where data is, what left, who saw what, signed;
- **controls**: tokenization, access rules, restriction, export and erasure for data subject requests.
